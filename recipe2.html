Nasi Lemak

Description

Nasi Lemak is a traditional Malaysian rice dish known for its fragrant coconut milk rice. It is commonly served with sambal, fried anchovies, peanuts, cucumber, and a boiled or fried egg. Its combination of creamy, spicy, salty, and crunchy flavors makes it one of Malaysia's most iconic dishes.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Jasmine rice 2 cups
Coconut milk 1 cup
Water 1 cup
Pandan leaves 2 leaves
Salt 1 teaspoon
Dried anchovies 100 g
Peanuts 100 g
Eggs 2
Cucumber 1
Sambal 4 tablespoons

Preparation

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Combine the rice, coconut milk, water, salt, and pandan leaves in a rice cooker or pot.
  3. Cook the rice until tender and allow it to rest for several minutes.
  4. Boil the eggs until fully cooked, then peel and cut them in half.
  5. Fry the anchovies until crispy and golden brown.
  6. Roast or fry the peanuts until lightly browned.
  7. Slice the cucumber into thin pieces.
  8. Prepare or heat the sambal until fragrant and ready to serve.
  9. Arrange the coconut rice with the anchovies, peanuts, cucumber, egg, and sambal.
  10. Serve immediately while the rice and toppings are warm.

Cooking Tips

  • Use good-quality coconut milk for a richer and more fragrant rice.
  • Do not use too much water because the coconut milk already provides additional liquid.
  • Toast or fry the peanuts and anchovies separately to keep them crunchy.
  • Balance the spicy sambal with the creamy coconut rice and fresh cucumber.

Recipe Details

Nasi Lemak Recipe Details
Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 30 minutes
Serving Size 2 servings
Serving Suggestions Serve with sambal, fried anchovies, peanuts, cucumber, and egg.
