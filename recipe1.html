Roti Canai

Description

Roti Canai is a popular Malaysian flatbread with South Asian influences. It is made from a soft dough that is stretched, folded, and pan-fried until flaky and golden. It is commonly served with dhal or curry and is especially popular as a breakfast or street food.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
All-purpose flour 250 g
Water 120 ml
Salt 1 teaspoon
Sugar 1 teaspoon
Condensed milk 1 tablespoon
Cooking oil 4 tablespoons
Butter 1 tablespoon
Egg 1

Preparation

  1. Combine the flour, salt, sugar, condensed milk, and egg in a bowl.
  2. Gradually add the water and mix until a soft dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough until it becomes smooth and elastic.
  4. Divide the dough into portions and coat each piece generously with oil.
  5. Cover the dough and let it rest for at least 2 hours.
  6. Stretch each portion of dough into a very thin sheet.
  7. Fold the dough several times to create layers.
  8. Flatten the folded dough gently into a round shape.
  9. Heat a pan and cook the roti with a small amount of butter or oil.
  10. Cook both sides until golden brown and flaky, then serve hot with curry.

Cooking Tips

  • Allow the dough to rest properly so it becomes easier to stretch.
  • Use enough oil during resting and stretching to create flaky layers.
  • Stretch the dough gently to avoid tearing it.
  • Cook over medium heat so the outside becomes golden without burning.

Recipe Details

Roti Canai Recipe Details
Preparation Time 2 hours 30 minutes
Cooking Time 15 minutes
Serving Size 4 servings
Serving Suggestions Serve hot with dhal, chicken curry, or beef curry.
