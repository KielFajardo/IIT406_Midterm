Rendang

Description

Rendang is a traditional slow-cooked meat dish associated with Malaysia and the wider Malay and Minangkabau culinary traditions. It is known for tender beef cooked with coconut milk, chilies, herbs, and aromatic spices. The long cooking process creates a rich, thick, and deeply flavored sauce that makes rendang a popular dish for special occasions and celebrations.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Beef chuck 500 g
Coconut milk 400 ml
Red chilies 5
Garlic cloves 4
Shallots 5
Lemongrass 2 stalks
Galangal 2 cm
Tamarind paste 1 tablespoon
Brown sugar 1 tablespoon
Cooking oil 2 tablespoons

Preparation

  1. Cut the beef into medium-sized pieces and set aside.
  2. Blend the chilies, garlic, shallots, galangal, and lemongrass into a spice paste.
  3. Heat the cooking oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  4. Fry the spice paste until fragrant and the oil begins to separate.
  5. Add the beef pieces and stir until they are evenly coated with the spice mixture.
  6. Pour in the coconut milk and bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.
  7. Add the tamarind paste and brown sugar, then stir thoroughly.
  8. Simmer the beef over low heat, stirring occasionally to prevent the sauce from sticking.
  9. Continue cooking until the beef becomes tender and the sauce becomes thick and dark.
  10. Serve the rendang hot with steamed rice.

Cooking Tips

  • Use beef with some fat and connective tissue because it becomes tender during slow cooking.
  • Cook the rendang slowly to allow the spices and coconut milk to develop a deeper flavor.
  • Stir occasionally near the end of cooking to prevent the thick sauce from burning.
  • Allow the sauce to reduce until it becomes thick and coats the beef.

Recipe Details

Rendang Recipe Details
Preparation Time 20 minutes
Cooking Time 2 hours
Serving Size 4 servings
Serving Suggestions Serve with steamed rice, cucumber, or traditional vegetable side dishes.
