Char Kway Teow

Description

Char Kway Teow is a popular Malaysian stir-fried noodle dish, especially associated with Penang. It is made with flat rice noodles, prawns, egg, bean sprouts, Chinese sausage, and soy sauce. Its smoky aroma, known as wok hei, makes it one of Malaysia's most recognizable noodle dishes.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Flat rice noodles 200 g
Prawns 100 g
Eggs 2
Bean sprouts 50 g
Garlic 2 cloves
Chinese sausage 50 g
Light soy sauce 1 tablespoon
Dark soy sauce 1 tablespoon
Chili paste 1 tablespoon
Cooking oil 1 tablespoon

Preparation

  1. Prepare the rice noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat a wok over high heat and add the cooking oil.
  3. Stir-fry the garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add the prawns and Chinese sausage, then cook until the prawns are nearly done.
  5. Push the ingredients to one side of the wok and crack in the eggs.
  6. Scramble the eggs until partially cooked.
  7. Add the rice noodles and toss everything together.
  8. Add the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and chili paste.
  9. Add the bean sprouts and stir-fry quickly over high heat.
  10. Serve immediately while hot.

Cooking Tips

  • Use high heat to create the smoky wok aroma known as wok hei.
  • Do not overcrowd the wok because the noodles may become soggy.
  • Prepare all ingredients before cooking because stir-frying happens quickly.
  • Avoid overcooking the bean sprouts so they remain slightly crunchy.

Recipe Details

Char Kway Teow Recipe Details
Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 10 minutes
Serving Size 2 servings
Serving Suggestions Serve hot with chili paste and lime.
