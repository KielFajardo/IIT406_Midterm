Laksa

Description

Laksa is a popular Malaysian noodle soup known for its rich, spicy, and aromatic broth. Different regions have their own versions, with ingredients such as coconut milk, chili, seafood, herbs, and noodles. Its combination of spicy, creamy, sour, and savory flavors makes laksa an important part of Malaysian cuisine.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Rice noodles 200 g
Coconut milk 400 ml
Prawns 150 g
Chicken stock 500 ml
Laksa paste 3 tablespoons
Bean sprouts 100 g
Fish sauce 1 tablespoon
Lime juice 2 tablespoons
Boiled eggs 2
Cooking oil 1 tablespoon

Preparation

  1. Prepare the rice noodles according to the package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  3. Add the laksa paste and fry until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the chicken stock and coconut milk, then bring the broth to a gentle simmer.
  5. Add the prawns and cook until they become pink and fully cooked.
  6. Season the broth with fish sauce and lime juice.
  7. Add the bean sprouts and cook briefly to keep them slightly crunchy.
  8. Place the cooked rice noodles into serving bowls.
  9. Pour the hot laksa broth and prawns over the noodles.
  10. Garnish with boiled egg and serve immediately.

Cooking Tips

  • Fry the laksa paste properly to release its full aroma before adding the liquid.
  • Do not overcook the prawns because they can become tough and rubbery.
  • Adjust the amount of laksa paste according to your preferred level of spiciness.
  • Add lime juice near the end of cooking to keep the broth fresh and slightly tangy.

Recipe Details

Laksa Recipe Details
Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 25 minutes
Serving Size 2 servings
Serving Suggestions Serve hot with boiled egg, bean sprouts, lime, and additional chili paste.
