Malaysia

Location: ~4°12′ N, 101°58′ E

Background

Malaysia is a multicultural country in Southeast Asia, with major Malay, Chinese, and Indian communities, as well as many Indigenous groups. Its culture has been influenced by Malay traditions, Islam, Chinese and Indian heritage, and its history of trade with other countries.

Cuisine

Malaysian cuisine is known for its bold, spicy, aromatic, and flavorful dishes. It combines Malay, Chinese, Indian, and Indigenous influences. Rice and noodles are common staples, while dishes often use spices, coconut milk, chili, and herbs.

Common Ingridients

Fun Facts: Malaysia is famous for its diverse mix of Malay, Chinese, and Indian flavors! They use sambal as well on most of their dishes to give it that kick!
